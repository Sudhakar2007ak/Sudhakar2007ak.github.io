About The Project

Making student analytics smarter with artificial intelligence.

The AI-Powered Student Performance & Career Prediction System is a machine learning-based application designed to help understand student performance and career possibilities. It analyzes academic marks, attendance, technical skills, communication skills, projects, certifications and learning habits. The system generates performance predictions, suggests potential career domains and identifies students who may require additional academic support. An interactive dashboard presents insights through visualizations and prediction results.
